ABOUT THIS PROJECT

A thoughtful home for what you read.

Book & Notes Vault is a lightweight personal library built with plain HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. It helps you keep book details and your own reading notes together, without needing an account or a server.

Made for curious minds. Your data stays in your browser's local storage until you export it or choose to clear it.
